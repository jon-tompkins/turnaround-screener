# FMC — FMC Corporation

52w range $10.56–$43.97 · Fwd P/E ~5.7 · P/B ~0.80 · EV/Rev ~1.7 · Rev TTM ~$3.43B
Screen mode: **basing** (~24% below 200-day MA) · Next earnings **July 29, 2026** (EPS est $0.22)

---

## Thesis in one paragraph

FMC is a 140-year-old global crop-protection company (insecticides, herbicides,
fungicides, plus biologicals and seed treatment) whose stock fell ~73% from
~$44 to ~$12 as a two-year industry-wide destocking cycle and soft pricing in
Latin America and Asia crushed volumes and earnings. It now trades at **~0.8x
book and ~5.7x forward earnings** on a real ~$3.4B-revenue franchise. The
asymmetric hook is that in **February 2026 the board authorized a formal
strategic review — explicitly including a sale of the entire company — and
retained financial and legal advisers.** In parallel, management is deleveraging
hard (~$1B debt-reduction target; definitive $252M sale of the India commercial
business to Crystal Crop). If the destocking cycle is bottoming (industry
signals say it is) and/or the sale process produces a bid, a name at 0.8x book
re-rates meaningfully. The offset is a heavy balance sheet (~$4.66B debt), a
guided-down Q2, and an explicitly "preliminary, no assurance" sale process. This
is a classic deep-value-with-catalyst cyclical distorted by leverage and washed-out
sentiment.

---

## Why this is interesting

### 1. A live, board-run sale process — not a rumor

On Feb 4–5, 2026, FMC's board authorized a formal process to explore strategic
options "including but not limited to the sale of the company," and has
**retained financial and legal advisers.** This is a real, disclosed process
run by the board, not activist speculation. At 0.8x book and ~1.7x EV/revenue, a
strategic or financial acquirer stepping in near even a modest premium to book
implies a stock materially above spot. Ag-chemistry is a consolidating industry
(Syngenta/ChemChina, Bayer/Monsanto, Corteva spin), so a strategic buyer for a
top-5 crop-protection portfolio is plausible.

### 2. Deep value on tangible metrics

- Forward P/E ~5.7 vs. a business that earned $1.20 in a single quarter (Q4 2025)
  before the cycle trough.
- Price/book ~0.80 — the market is valuing the equity below the accounting value
  of the net assets of a franchise with real patents, registrations and channel.
- EV/revenue ~1.7 for a specialty-chemistry business that historically ran
  low-to-mid-20s EBITDA margins.

### 3. Aggressive, credible deleveraging

Management has a stated plan to pay down **~$1B of debt** through asset sales and
licensing. The **India commercial business sale to Crystal Crop Protection for
$252M** (announced May 7, 2026; expected to close by year-end) is the first
concrete step and is separate from — and does not impede — the strategic review.

### 4. The destocking cycle appears to be bottoming

Crop-protection earnings have been dragged for two years by channel destocking.
Industry commentary now points to inventories normalizing toward just-in-time
purchasing, with a modest 2026 volume recovery expected to more than offset
ongoing pricing pressure. FMC has been aligning shipments with true in-field
demand; when the channel refills, the operating leverage on a fixed cost base is
significant.

### 5. Sentiment is washed out and Q1 already beat

Q1 2026 came in at reported −$0.23 vs −$0.38 estimate (~+40% surprise). The sell
side sits at "hold" with a **$15.84 mean target (~35% above spot)**, high $26,
low $12 across 14 analysts. Expectations are low; the bar to surprise is low.

---

## Why this could fail

### 1. The balance sheet is the whole ballgame

~$4.66B total debt against ~$391M cash and **negative TTM operating cash flow
(−$138.5M)**. Debt/equity ~252. In a leveraged cyclical, if the volume recovery
slips a couple of quarters, interest expense and covenants — not the P&L — drive
the equity. This is why the stock trades below book: the market is pricing real
financial risk, not just pessimism.

### 2. Q2 2026 was guided down hard

Management guided Q2 EPS to **$0.16–0.26 vs ~$0.45 consensus** and revenue to
**$850–900M vs ~$976M** expected. Demand normalization is a work in progress,
not a completed turnaround. The July 29 print could confirm the guide-down and
pressure the "basing" technical.

### 3. "Preliminary … no assurance"

The strategic review is explicitly at a preliminary stage with "no assurance
that the process will result in any transaction." Sale processes in leveraged
cyclicals frequently end with no deal (bidders wait for a cheaper entry) or a
price that disappoints holders who anchored to book. If the process closes with
"we've decided to remain independent," the stock likely round-trips lower.

### 4. Pricing pressure persists

Volume recovery is being partly offset by soft pricing in LatAm and APAC.
Deeply negative TTM profit margins reflect impairments/writedowns; a clean,
sustained return to profitability is not yet visible on the tape.

### 5. Basing, not recovering

Screen mode is "basing" — the bottom is unconfirmed. The stock is ~24% below its
200-day MA. A disappointing earnings/guide could trigger a re-break of the
$10.56 low.

## Position sizing

Treat as a **small-to-medium value/event position: ~1.5–2.5% of a speculative
book.** This is not a "back up the truck" name — the leverage means the bear case
is a real ~30%+ drawdown, and the bull case leans partly on an uncertain M&A
process. Size so that the bear outcome is a tolerable hit, and let the sale-process
optionality be a free-ish call on top of the cyclical bottom.

## Entry plan

- **Starter** at $11.50–12.00 (spot, near current).
- **Add** into $10.60–11.00 if it retests the 52-week low WITHOUT a fundamental
  break (i.e., weakness on tape, not on a blown guide or a collapsed sale process).
- Prefer to have the starter on before the **July 29 earnings** so you own the
  strategic-review optionality, but keep dry powder for post-print volatility.
- Options alternative: a **Jan 2027 $12/$15 call spread** expresses the
  sale-process/re-rating thesis with defined risk and avoids owning the leveraged
  equity outright (ATM IV ~25%, options reasonably liquid: ~9,000 call OI).

## Exit plan

**On success:**
- Trim into a confirmed sale/strategic transaction announcement (event is the exit;
  don't hold for the last few percent of arb spread).
- Absent a deal, scale out $15–16 (mean-target zone) and $18–20 (book-value re-rate).

**On failure:**
- Hard stop on thesis break: a strategic-review termination ("remaining
  independent") with no offsetting deleveraging progress → exit, don't average down.
- Technical: a decisive weekly close below **$10.20** (below the $10.56 low) on
  a blown earnings guide → cut.
- Fundamental: a covenant/refinancing scare or a debt-reduction target walk-back.

## Hedges (for a larger position)

- Pair against a healthier ag-input peer (e.g. short a basket / long FMC) to
  isolate the FMC-specific catalyst from sector beta.
- For an options-expressed position, the call spread is self-hedging (defined risk).

A leveraged, deeply-discounted (0.8x book) crop-protection cyclical with a live
board-run sale process and a bottoming industry cycle — worth a small-to-medium

asymmetric kicker.
