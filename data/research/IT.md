# IT — Gartner, Inc. — Deep Dive (2026-07-28)

**Thesis (one paragraph):** Gartner is a high-margin, subscription-based research and advisory franchise that de-rated hard in H1 2026 on a genuinely important fear: that agentic AI lets enterprises substitute cheap AI assistants for Gartner's pricey seat-based research, collapsing its contract-value (CV) growth. CV did slow to ~1% YoY, and the stock sits ~17% below its 200-day MA. But the near-term tape has flipped: Q1 2026 adjusted EPS beat by ~11% and shares jumped ~10.7% on the print, management *raised* EBITDA/EPS/FCF guidance, and momentum is strongly positive into the August 4 Q2 report (+5.1% 1d, +9.9% 5d). This is a "quality compounder on sale, with a hard binary catalyst" setup. The entire question is whether CV inflects back toward Gartner's 12-16% long-term target — or whether AI substitution keeps it stuck near 1%. Own a spot starter and express the Aug 4 catalyst with a small, defined-risk options leg; the stock has already rallied into the print, so respect sell-the-news risk.

---

## Why this is interesting

1. **Momentum has already turned ahead of a hard catalyst.** The basing pattern is resolving upward (price back above the 100d MA, strong 1d/5d moves) right into the August 4 Q2 print. That combination — improving tape + discrete near-term event — is exactly the setup the screener is built to surface.

2. **Q1 was a genuine beat-and-raise.** Adjusted EPS $3.32 beat by ~11% and management raised full-year adjusted EPS, EBITDA, and FCF guidance. In a name where the bear thesis is "estimates must come down," a raise is a meaningful counter-signal.

3. **The franchise economics are excellent.** Gartner is a sticky, high-retention subscription business with strong FCF conversion and a long buyback track record. Even modest CV reacceleration drops through to outsized EPS given operating leverage and shrinking share count.

4. **Management is explicit that CV will accelerate.** CEO Gene Hall and CFO Craig Safian have repeatedly reaffirmed the long-term 12-16% CV growth target and guided to acceleration in H2 2026. Aug 4 is the first real test of that promise.

5. **AI can be a tailwind, not just a threat.** Gartner's own research pegs enormous AI spend (global AI spending projected toward the multi-trillions; $64B on AI models/platforms in 2026), and enterprises navigating AI adoption are exactly the buyers of Gartner's advisory. If management reframes AI as a demand driver on Aug 4, the disruption discount compresses.

6. **Listed, tradable options** allow a defined-risk expression of the binary catalyst (Aug 21 / Sep 18 calls), with modest IV keeping premium reasonable.

---

## Why this could fail

1. **The AI-substitution thesis is structural, not cyclical.** Gartner itself forecasts agentic AI could disrupt up to ~$234B of enterprise application software spend by 2030 and pressure seat-based pricing. If clients genuinely swap Gartner seats for AI tooling, CV growth stays depressed and the moat erodes permanently — no multiple re-rate saves that.

2. **CV is still only ~1%.** The reacceleration to 12-16% is a *promise*, not a result. Q1 CV limped +1% and revenue actually slipped ~1.5%. The profitability beat was margin/cost-led, not growth-led — a lower-quality kind of beat.

3. **Stock has rallied into the print → sell-the-news risk.** After +9.9% in five days, the bar is higher. A merely in-line Q2 (CV still ~1-3%, no clear acceleration) could trigger a sharp fade, and the options leg would decay fast.

4. **Full-year top-line guide was trimmed** (revenue below ~$6.41B) even as profit guidance rose. The market ultimately pays Gartner for CV/revenue growth, not just cost control.

5. **Governance probe headline risk.** Recent reporting paired the Q2 anticipation with a governance-probe news item — a wildcard that can inject volatility unrelated to fundamentals.

6. **Dispersed, falling targets.** Average target ~$162 (+~22%) but Wells Fargo cut to $120 — the Street disagrees sharply on whether CV inflects, so post-print revisions could cut either way.

## Entry plan

- **Spot tranche 1 (now):** ~$134, small starter.
- **Options (pre-Aug 4):** small Aug 21/Sep 18 call position only if IV stays modest; skip the options leg entirely if the stock keeps ripping into the print (risk/reward deteriorates as it front-runs the catalyst).
- **Add zone:** post-print, add spot on a *confirming* CV acceleration even on a gap-up.

---

## Exit plan

- **Success triggers (trim/let run):**
  - Move to the ~$162-165 average-target zone → trim the spot half; sell/roll the calls into strength.
  - CV growth accelerating for two consecutive quarters toward the 12-16% target → hold the core.
- **Failure triggers (cut):**
  - Q2 CV still ~1% with no acceleration and guide not raised → exit spot on the fade; the options leg is likely a write-off (accept the defined loss).
  - Governance-probe escalation into a material disclosure → reassess independent of fundamentals.
  - Break back below the 100d MA on heavy post-earnings volume → respect the tape.

## Hedges (for a larger position)

- The options chain enables clean event hedging: a **put spread** around Aug 4, or structuring the bullish view as a **call spread** to cap premium given the pre-print rally.
- If holding a larger spot position through the print, a near-dated protective put financed by an upside call sale (collar) caps both tails around the binary.

## Watch items / hard dates

- **Q2 2026 earnings — August 4, 2026 (pre-market).** The event. Watch: **contract value growth** (the single most important number — is it accelerating off ~1%?), revenue trajectory, any *further* raise to adjusted EPS/EBITDA/FCF, and management's framing of AI (threat vs. demand driver).
- **Consensus:** ~$3.77 adj. EPS. A beat is likely on cost control; the market reaction hinges on CV, not EPS.
- **Analyst target dispersion** (~$120 Wells Fargo to ~$162 average) — watch post-print revisions as the real verdict.
- **AI-disruption data points** — any client-churn or seat-reduction commentary would validate the bear case; any "clients hire us to navigate AI" framing supports the bull case.
