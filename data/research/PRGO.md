## Why This Could Fail

1. **$3.83B debt at 2.6x market cap makes equity a subordinated call option.** At ~7.6x EV/EBITDA the enterprise value is reasonable ($4.88B), but any EBITDA contraction or multiple compression leaves almost nothing for equity. Debt is the thesis-killer.

2. **Organic revenue declining -11% in Q1 2026 is alarming.** Core net sales -8.3%, organic -11%. This is not "revenue stabilizing" — this is a business under real volume pressure. Category headwinds (infant formula exit, retailer destocking) may persist longer than management expects.

3. **Interim leadership during complex restructuring is risky.** The Three-S plan is multi-year and requires consistent execution across U.S. and European segments. A permanent CEO search during this execution window introduces real operational risk.

4. **"Spring" maturity covenant could accelerate revolver maturity.** If the 4.900% Senior Notes due 2030 are not extended/refinanced before they hit a 91-day trigger window, the revolver matures early. This isn't imminent but needs monitoring as 2029 approaches.

5. **High short interest means the shorts have conviction — they may be right.** With 27% of float short, serious institutional money has reviewed this story and made a bet against it. Short interest this high often reflects knowledge of structural problems that aren't visible in headline adj EPS.
