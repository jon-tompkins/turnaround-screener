# Charter Communications (CHTR) — Deep Dive

*Analyst note, 2026-08-03. Screen mode: basing. Price at analysis ~$146.51; 52-week range $111.55–$285.82.*

## Thesis (one paragraph)

Charter (Spectrum) has been halved from ~$286 to the $110s as the market extrapolates broadband subscriber losses — 172k internet subs lost in Q2 2026 — into terminal decline for cable. That fear has pushed the stock to roughly **3x forward earnings** on a business still generating multi-billion-dollar free cash flow. The bull case is a **free-cash-flow inflection**: capex rolls off hard (from ~$11.4B in 2026 to $7.5–8.0B by 2028–29 as the network-evolution and rural build complete), the Cox merger (~$34.5B, closing mid-to-late August 2026, ~$1B run-rate synergies) adds scale, and Spectrum Mobile keeps compounding (+406k lines in Q2, >12.5M total). Management guides FCF/share from ~$33 toward ~$64 as capex falls and the share count shrinks. Against that: broadband is still bleeding, leverage is ~$96B (4.18x, targeting 3.5x post-Cox), and buybacks are paused near-term to prioritize deleveraging. This is a deep-value, catalyst-rich convergence play for patient capital, not a momentum trade.

## Why this is interesting

1. **FCF inflection is the whole story and it's mechanical.** Capex guided down from ~$11.4B (2026) → ~$9.5B (2027) → $7.5–8.0B (2028–29) vs $11.7B in 2025. Management frames future FCF at ~$9.1B (vs ~$4.7B TTM) and FCF/share rising from ~$33 to ~$64 — driven by lower capex, EBITDA growth, and a shrinking share count. At a ~$146 price that is an eventual sub-3x P/FCF.
2. **Cox merger closes imminently.** The ~$34.5B Cox transaction is expected to close **mid-to-late August 2026**, with run-rate synergies approaching ~$1B. A hard, near-term catalyst that reshapes scale and the deleveraging path.
3. **Convergence is working.** Spectrum Mobile added 406k lines in Q2 (16% / ~1.7M growth over 12 months); video losses narrowed to just 21k. Mobile bundling is the retention/ARPU defense against fixed-wireless, and it is compounding.
4. **Management credibility reset.** CEO Chris Winfrey publicly called prior retention offers "too aggressive," set a post-Cox leverage target of a flat **3.5x** (down from 4.18x) within three years, and paused buybacks to prioritize debt reduction — a shift from subsidy-driven churn toward disciplined pricing and balance-sheet repair.
5. **Insider buying at the lows.** Net +1.6M shares purchased over six months (19 buy transactions) as the stock washed out — insiders leaning in near multi-year lows.
6. **Optionality on structure.** Analysts are openly floating a Comcast–Charter combination, and Charter is in talks (incl. SpaceX/Starlink) to bundle satellite/mobile as a broadband defense — low-probability but real upside optionality.

## Why this could fail

1. **Broadband still shrinking, not troughing.** 172k internet losses in Q2 deteriorated YoY and sequentially. Fiber overbuild, T-Mobile/Verizon fixed wireless (FWA), and Starlink are all taking share. If losses accelerate, the EBITDA base that funds the FCF story erodes faster than capex falls.
2. **Leverage is enormous.** ~$96.7B total debt vs $509M cash; net debt/EBITDA 4.18x. This is a levered equity — modest EBITDA declines or higher-for-longer rates compress the equity disproportionately, and the 3.5x target is a three-year journey.
3. **EBITDA is flat-to-down.** 2026 EBITDA likely ~-1% YoY even with ad tailwinds; the thesis leans entirely on capex-driven FCF, not growth. If the FCF ramp slips (integration costs, capex stickiness), the re-rating stalls.
4. **Buybacks paused.** The share-count-shrink engine — a core part of the FCF/share math — is on hold through Q3 to fund deleveraging and the Cox close. Near-term the per-share tailwind is muted.
5. **Sell-side still cutting.** Wells Fargo, Bernstein/SocGen, RBC (to ~$150) all trimmed targets on broadband weakness. The tape has been "cheap and getting cheaper."
6. **Bounce already underway.** Stock popped ~12% in five days into this analysis after the washout; entering now chases the reflex rally off the low.

## Valuation & EV math

- ~3.3x forward P/E, ~3.8x trailing; P/FCF ~4x TTM and heading lower if the ~$9.1B FCF guide is achieved.
- The debt dominates EV ($118.4B EV vs $19.7B market cap), so equity value is highly geared to (a) EBITDA stability and (b) the capex-down FCF ramp. Small improvements in broadband trend + realized synergies move the equity a lot.

### Probability-weighted scenarios (12–24 month horizon, from ~$146.51)

| Scenario | Prob | What happens | Target | Return |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bull | 30% | Broadband losses trough within ~2–3 quarters, Cox synergies land, capex rolls off, FCF/share ramps toward the low-$40s and buybacks resume; multiple re-rates to ~6–7x FCF | ~$230 | +57% |
| Base | 45% | Broadband keeps bleeding modestly but mobile + capex-down still lift FCF; market slowly credits deleveraging; stock re-rates part-way | ~$180 | +23% |
| Bear | 25% | Broadband losses accelerate, EBITDA falls >2–3%, deleveraging stalls; equity re-breaks toward the lows | ~$105 | -28% |

Expected value ≈ 0.30(+57%) + 0.45(+23%) + 0.25(-28%) ≈ **+21% EV**, with meaningfully positive skew from the mechanical capex/FCF ramp and the imminent Cox catalyst. The distribution is wide because of leverage — this is a high-variance deep-value name.

## Position sizing

- **Starter: ~1.5–2.5% of portfolio** at current levels, with room to add. The FCF inflection + hard Cox catalyst justify a slightly larger starter than a typical basing name, but leverage caps how big this should get.
- Add on a pullback toward the **$120s**, or after the Cox close de-risks the structure.

## Entry plan

- Starter now (~$146) given the imminent Cox catalyst, but keep it partial — the stock already bounced ~12%.
- Preferred add levels: **$120–130** (retest of the washout zone) or a post-Cox-close dip on "sell-the-news."
- For those who prefer defined risk, **Jan 2027 / Jan 2028 calls** are viable — options are deeply liquid (15 expiries, real ATM IV ~3.1) — to express the FCF-ramp thesis with capped downside.

## Exit plan

- **Success:** trim into **$200–230** (mid-single-digit P/FCF on ramped FCF, ~6–7x); hold a runner if broadband has clearly troughed and buybacks have resumed.
- **Failure triggers (cut):** (a) broadband net losses accelerate for two consecutive quarters, (b) mobile net-adds decelerate sharply, (c) leverage rises instead of trending toward 3.5x, or (d) capex guidance slips upward. Any two → exit.

## Hedges

- Given the leverage, size-ups should be paired with a hedge: long-dated OTM puts below ~$110, or a paired short against a fiber/FWA beneficiary (a large telecom with FWA momentum) to isolate the cable-specific FCF thesis from the broadband-share secular trade.

## Watch items / catalyst dates

- **Cox merger close — mid-to-late August 2026** (structure, synergy cadence, pro-forma leverage).
- **Q3 2026 earnings (late October 2026)** — broadband net-add trajectory (trough or not), mobile momentum, capex/FCF progression, buyback resumption.
- **Monthly FWA net-add trends** at T-Mobile/Verizon and **Starlink** capacity — the leading tell on broadband pressure.
- **Leverage trajectory** toward the 3.5x post-Cox target.
- **Comcast–Charter M&A chatter** and any SpaceX/Starlink bundling deal — optionality catalysts.

## One-sentence call

A halved, hated, heavily-levered cable operator trading at ~3x earnings into a mechanical capex-down free-cash-flow ramp and an imminent Cox close — worth a partial starter now with adds into weakness, sized with respect for the $96B debt load.
