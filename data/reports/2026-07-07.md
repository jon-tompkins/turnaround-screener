### GOGO — Gogo Inc. `[basing]`

*Communication Services / Telecom Services*

**Setup:** Stock is down ~70% from its 2022 peak as Starlink's vertically-integrated, lower-cost aviation Wi-Fi push structurally erodes Gogo's legacy business aviation connectivity moat, with revenue basically flat-to-down and a heavy debt load left over from the pre-LEO-competition era.

**Catalyst:** Q2 earnings — first look at whether Galileo/LEO STC ramp and the Satcom Direct integration are offsetting legacy revenue erosion (2026-08-06 earnings)

**Suggested trade:** Skip, or at most a small spot position sized for the leverage risk — not an options trade given the binary structural threat from Starlink

**Conviction:** 4/10 | Upside est: 60% | Downside est: 45%

**Bullish:**
- Won a 5-year sole-source federal government satcom contract and got FAA/EASA certification for its own LEO-based Galileo system (300+ antennas shipped, 99 activations, 31 STCs done with 20 more expected in H1 2026)
- Acquired Satcom Direct (~$375M cash + 5M shares) to bulk up scale and product breadth against Starlink rather than cede the market passively
- Forward PE of only 4.9x if FY2026 guidance ($922M revenue, $0.67 EPS) holds — priced for continued decline, not for stabilization
- EV/EBITDA of 7.2x is not demanding for a business still generating positive FCF ($24.5M) and operating cash flow ($84.8M)
- Director bought 250k shares in the open market in March 2026 around $4.54-4.57 — a real insider bet near current levels

**Bearish:**
- Total debt of ~$903M against a $531M market cap is a genuinely dangerous leverage profile for a company facing a structural competitive threat, not a cyclical one
- Starlink is vertically integrated (owns the satellites) and undercutting on price — Gogo can certify more STCs but can't out-cost SpaceX's economics
- Revenue growth is negative (-1.7% ttm) even before accounting for the dilutive Satcom Direct deal financing
- Only 2 analysts cover the name and neither issued a strong buy — thin coverage means limited independent validation of the bull case
- Q1 2026 revenue missed estimates ($226.3M vs $233.3M) even as EPS beat, suggesting the top-line erosion is already showing up

**Key risk:** Starlink continues taking business aviation share faster than Gogo's LEO buildout can offset, and the debt load leaves no room for a slow bleed
