*Communication Services / Telecom Services*

**Catalyst:** Sequential improvement in broadband net-adds (or a clear trough) plus continued mobile growth and buyback-driven EPS accretion in the Q3 2026 print (Q3 2026 earnings (late October 2026); watch monthly fixed-wireless net-add trends as a leading tell)

**Suggested trade:** Spot starter now with room to add on a pullback toward the $120s, or defined-risk longer-dated calls (Jan 2027/2028) given liquid options and the FCF/buyback tailwind. Deep-value convergence play, not a fast mover.

**Conviction:** 6/10 | Upside est: 70% | Downside est: 30%

**Bullish:**
- Q2 2026 EPS beat handily ($10.66 vs $10.14 est) on revenue of $13.53B (slightly above consensus) despite the top-line dip
- Mobile is the growth engine: +406k Spectrum Mobile lines (>12.5M total) and video losses narrowed to just 21k — convergence bundle is working
- Enormous FCF (~$2.2B TTM) funding aggressive buybacks; at ~3.3x forward P/E every dollar of FCF retires a large slug of a shrinking share count
- Insider buying: net +1.6M shares over 6 months (19 purchase transactions) near multi-year lows
- Management credibility reset — CEO Winfrey called prior retention offers 'too aggressive' and set a 3.5x leverage target, signaling a shift from subsidy-driven churn to disciplined pricing
- Deeply liquid options (15 expiries, real ATM IV ~3.1) enable defined-risk expressions

**Bearish:**
- Core broadband still shrinking — 172k internet subs lost in Q2, worse YoY; the secular competitive pressure is real and not yet stabilized
- Massive leverage: ~$96.7B total debt vs $509M cash; a levered equity where multiple compression + rate risk cut both ways
- Revenue declined 1.7% YoY; the bull case relies on FCF/buybacks, not growth
- Stock already popped ~12% in 5 days post-earnings, so near-term entry chases the bounce off the washout low
- Sell-side still trimming targets (e.g., RBC to $150) even while acknowledging the FCF story

**Key risk:** Broadband subscriber losses accelerate rather than trough as fixed-wireless and fiber keep taking share, overwhelming mobile gains and stalling the FCF-per-share thesis while $96B of debt caps the multiple
