### CNXC — Concentrix Corporation `[basing]`

*Technology / Information Technology Services*

**Setup:** Concentrix, a global customer-experience (CX/BPO) outsourcer, has been crushed (~55% below its 52w high) by fears that GenAI automates away the human call-center work at its core, plus a messy ~$4.5-5.5B-net-debt Webhelp integration and a $1.5B goodwill write-down. The deep-value thesis: it still gushes cash (~$775M FCF on a ~$1.6B market cap) and trades at fwd P/E ~2.2 and 0.58x book, so even flat execution is heavily de-risked at the price.

**Catalyst:** Q3 2026 earnings on Sep 24 — the market needs evidence that the higher-value tech/GenAI contract mix is offsetting seat erosion and that FCF guidance holds; sustained FCF + debt paydown at this multiple is the re-rating driver, while any FCF cut or contract losses confirm the AI-disruption bear. (Q3 2026 earnings, 2026-09-24 (after close, ~8 weeks out))

**Suggested trade:** Small spot starter position only, sized for the possibility of a value trap — this is a levered, secularly-challenged deep-value name where the FCF yield pays you to wait but the debt + AI-to-BPO threat cap conviction. Prefer spot over options (illiquid). Existing research note at data/research/CNXC.md

**Conviction:** 6/10 | Upside est: 55% | Downside est: 35%

**Bullish:**
- Extreme cash generation vs price: ~$775M FCF / ~$1.58B market cap = ~49% FCF yield; 2026 adjusted FCF guided $630-650M; returned $258M to shareholders and cut net debt $184M
- Deeply cheap: fwd P/E ~2.2, EV/EBITDA 5.3, P/B 0.58, EV/Revenue 0.68 — priced for terminal decline
- CEO buying in the open market (Caldwell added shares at $21.25 in July and $26.97 in March) — modest but a real insider signal
- Webhelp 'met synergy goals' per management; the worst of the integration/impairment may be behind it
- Pivoting toward higher-value GenAI/SaaS-enabled CX contracts rather than pure headcount call centers

**Bearish:**
- The AI threat cuts directly at the business model: GenAI/agentic support automates the exact seat-based work Concentrix sells — this is the disruptee, not the disruptor
- Heavy leverage: ~$5.5B total debt, debt/equity ~204, EV ~$6.8B dwarfs the ~$1.6B equity — most enterprise value is debt, so equity is a levered call on execution
- Growth has evaporated: 2026 guide reset to just 0.25-1.25% cc revenue growth; profit margins are negative TTM (-13%) after the write-down
- Big holder selling: Groupe Bruxelles Lambert dumped 6.0M shares (~$133M) at $22.25 in April — net insider shares sold -35.7%, swamping the CEO's small buys
- Options are effectively illiquid for this name (thin OI, unreliable IV) — hard to structure leverage cleanly
- Still basing below the 200d MA; no confirmed bottom

**Key risk:** GenAI automates CX seats faster than Concentrix can move up-market, so FCF and contract volume decline while the ~$5.5B debt load stays fixed — the classic levered value trap

### EPAM — EPAM Systems, Inc. `[basing]`

*Technology / Information Technology Services*

**Setup:** EPAM is down ~55-62% YTD and ~75% from its 2021 peak on the market's fear that AI code assistants structurally shrink demand for outsourced digital engineering (billable hours deflation), compounded by softer North American IT budgets. The turnaround thesis is that EPAM is a high-quality, net-cash, still-growing engineering firm that is repositioning as an AI-delivery partner rather than an AI victim, at a valuation (fwd P/E ~7) that already prices in secular decline.

**Catalyst:** Q2 2026 earnings on Aug 6 (pre-market) — a hard, near-term binary. The market wants proof that AI-native revenue is scaling and that North American demand isn't deteriorating further; a beat + stable/raised guide could snap a heavily shorted (25.7% of float), beaten-down stock higher, while any guidance cut confirms the structural bear. (Q2 2026 earnings, 2026-08-06 (pre-market, ~8 days out))

**Suggested trade:** Starter spot position now (small-to-medium) ahead of Aug 6. For leverage on the binary, small Jan 2027 $110-120 calls sized as a defined-risk bet — ATM IV ~53% is elevated into earnings, so keep options tranche small and prefer adding spot on any post-print dip if the thesis holds

**Conviction:** 7/10 | Upside est: 60% | Downside est: 30%

**Bullish:**
- Fortress balance sheet: ~$1.04B cash vs ~$288M debt (net cash), current ratio 2.67 — rare among cheap turnarounds; funds buybacks without leverage risk
- Still growing and profitable: revenue +7.6% YoY, 6.96% net margin, ~$693M FCF; Q1 beat ($2.86 vs $2.75) and raised full-year EPS outlook to $12.98-$13.28
- Cheap on multiple axes: fwd P/E ~7.1, EV/EBITDA 6.1, EV/Revenue 0.81, P/B 1.54 — trough-ish for a franchise engineering name
- AI positioning is offense, not just defense: >$125M AI-native revenue/quarter, multi-year Anthropic partnership (Claude practice targeting 10,000+ certified architects, 5,000 by Q3), positions EPAM as an AI implementation arms dealer
- Aggressive capital return: completed ~$547M buyback (~3M shares) in May, shrinking the float into the drawdown
- Analyst targets well above spot: mean ~$135 vs ~$100; Stifel $246, Mizuho/Guggenheim ~$200 — Buy/Outperform reiterated even after cuts

**Bearish:**
- Core secular risk is real and unresolved: if Claude Code / AI assistants collapse billable-hours demand faster than EPAM can shift to outcome-based AI delivery, revenue and margins erode structurally, not cyclically
- Guidance is decelerating and was CUT: 2026 revenue growth trimmed to 4.0-6.5% (2.5-5.0% organic cc); management flags weak North American visibility and 'less predictable' client spend
- Q2 guide implies only ~4% YoY growth at midpoint ($1.400-1.415B) — momentum is soft into the print
- The AI partnership is strategically sound but unproven as a margin/growth engine; 'positioning is highly uncertain' per bearish analysts
- Insiders are not buying (only director stock-award grants); no conviction signal from management purchases
- Basing, not recovering: still 30% below its 200d MA, so no confirmed trend — could re-break down on a soft print

**Key risk:** Aug 6 guidance cut confirming AI-driven demand destruction — organic growth stalling toward zero would validate the structural bear and re-rate the stock lower despite the cheap multiple
