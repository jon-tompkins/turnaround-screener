### CHTR — Charter Communications, Inc. `[basing]`

*Communication Services / Telecom Services*

**Setup:** Charter, the #2 US cable/broadband operator (Spectrum), collapsed ~49% from $286 to ~$146 as broadband subscriber losses accelerated (172k internet losses in Q2, worse than Q1's 120k) under fixed-wireless and fiber attack. The setup is deep-value: 3.3x forward earnings, ~$2.2B FCF, a $34.5B Cox merger closing now, and conspicuous insider buying well above the current price.

**Catalyst:** Cox Communications merger close and integration/synergy realization; stabilization of broadband net-adds via mobile-convergence bundling (Cox close expected mid-to-late August 2026 (imminent); Q3 earnings 2026-10-30)

**Suggested trade:** Starter-to-medium spot position with a plan to add on Cox-close clarity. For leverage, consider Jan 2027 calls given liquid options; size for the balance-sheet risk (this is a leveraged-equity turnaround, not a safe compounder).

**Conviction:** 6/10 | Upside est: 60% | Downside est: 35%

**Bullish:**
- Extreme cheapness: trailing P/E 3.8, forward P/E 3.3, EV/EBITDA 5.4, P/B ~1.0 — priced for terminal decline
- Hard near-term catalyst: $34.5B Cox acquisition expected to close mid-to-late August 2026 with ~$800M-$1B expense synergies
- Aggressive insider buying with real money: CEO Winfrey bought ~$1.2M at $172, directors bought at $140-175 (all above spot); net +1.58M shares over 6 months
- Convergence working: mobile penetration of internet base hit 20% and internet+mobile customers churn ~40% less — the strategic answer to the broadband bleed
- Very high short interest (50.4% of float, 7.18 days to cover) sets up squeeze potential on any positive surprise
- Beat Q2 EPS ($10.66 vs $10.06 est); still generates large operating cash flow ($16.5B)

**Bearish:**
- Enormous leverage: ~$96.7B debt, debt/equity ~442, current ratio 0.36 — equity is a thin sliver on the EV, so small EBITDA moves swing the stock hard
- Broadband losses are accelerating, not stabilizing (120k Q1 to 172k Q2); CEO admitted retention strategy was 'too aggressive'
- Revenue declining (-1.7% YoY) and EBITDA ex-Cox down 3.2% — the core is shrinking
- Fixed wireless (T-Mobile/Verizon) and fiber overbuild are structural, not cyclical, competitive threats
- Cox integration adds execution and near-term cost risk on top of an already stretched balance sheet
- Consensus is Hold; the wide target range ($101-$380, mean $184) reflects genuine disagreement on terminal value

**Key risk:** Broadband subscriber losses keep accelerating and fixed-wireless/fiber permanently impair the cash-flow base, so the low multiple is a value trap rather than a discount given the ~$97B debt load.

*Healthcare / Medical Devices*

**Catalyst:** Continued Inspire V rollout / adoption data and any resolution of the reimbursement overhang; secondary optionality from an acquisition by a larger medtech (Q3 earnings 2026-11-02; SLEEP-conference and rollout data through 2026)

**Suggested trade:** Spot only, small starter. Scale on confirmation above the 200-day MA; net-cash balance sheet and takeout optionality provide a floor.

**Conviction:** 5/10 | Upside est: 55% | Downside est: 30%

- Real, profitable business: 86% gross margins, positive FCF (~$95M), ~$321M cash vs only ~$29M debt (net cash), current ratio 8.5
- Beat EPS by 138-157% three quarters running (Aug: +0.14 vs -0.25 est) — profitability far better than the reset guidance implied
- Net insider buying over 6 months (+216k shares net, 24 purchase transactions) against a beaten-down tape
- Inspire V next-gen data strong: implant time -20%, median AHI 34.4 to 8.4, no revisions/explants in early cohort; possible medtech acquisition target (Trefis floated buyer speculation)
- Deeply oversold on long-term basis (34.9% of 200-week MA) while 30-day momentum has turned up (+17%)

**Bearish:**
- Secular overhang: GLP-1 weight-loss drugs reduce OSA severity and the implant-eligible population; this is a genuine, hard-to-quantify threat
- Revenue is actually declining (-7.6% TTM) and FY26 growth guide is only 4-10% — this is a decelerating, not accelerating, story
- Forward P/E ~37.6 vs trailing 12.7 signals the market expects a big earnings step-down; not cheap on forward numbers

- Options are effectively illiquid (total OI ~126 calls / ~161 puts) — no clean way to express via options
- Analyst mean target ($60.4) is barely above spot; consensus rating is Hold — no Street conviction for a big move

**Key risk:** GLP-1-driven structural shrinkage of the OSA implant market makes the growth reset permanent rather than cyclical, capping any multiple re-rating.
