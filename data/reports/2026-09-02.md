### CHTR — Charter Communications, Inc. `[basing]`

*Communication Services / Telecom Services*

**Setup:** Charter, the #2 US cable/broadband operator, collapsed ~65% from a ~$422 peak to ~$150 as it lost broadband subscribers to fixed-wireless and fiber competitors (120k lost in Q1 2026, 172k in Q2). It is basing while it integrates the $34.5B Cox acquisition and leans on mobile-broadband convergence to stem the bleed.

**Catalyst:** Broadband sub losses stabilizing plus visible Cox synergy capture and convergence-driven ARPU/retention gains (Q3-Q4 2026 earnings and 2027 as Cox integration progresses)

**Suggested trade:** Starter spot near $150 as a deep-value FCF/convergence call; add on evidence broadband losses are bottoming.

**Conviction:** 5/10 | Upside est: 55% | Downside est: 25%

**Bullish:**
- Massive free cash flow and a deeply discounted multiple; the Street mean target implies ~67% upside from current levels
- Cox merger (closed 2026) creates the largest US internet/video provider at 37M customers, with meaningful synergy and scale in mobile convergence
- Mobile is the growth engine — 12M+ Spectrum Mobile lines and rising; free-mobile-line offers are a real retention/bundling lever
- 23.5% operating margin and 9% net margin — this is a cash machine, not a distressed operator; buybacks shrink the float

**Bearish:**
- Core broadband is in secular decline; subscriber losses are accelerating (172k in Q2 vs 120k in Q1), which is the crux of the bear case
- Enormous leverage: ~$96.7B total debt and 441% D/E — refinancing at higher rates pressures the equity
- Cox integration is large and complex; synergy realization and merger timing remain execution risks
- Fixed-wireless (T-Mobile/Verizon) and fiber overbuilders are structural, well-funded competitors that are not going away

**Key risk:** Broadband subscriber erosion keeps accelerating, so FCF compresses faster than debt paydown and buybacks can offset
